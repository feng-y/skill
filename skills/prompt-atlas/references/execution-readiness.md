# Execution readiness

Use this reference only when the contract must drive direct or autonomous work. Intent Take owns protection selection and the semantic commitments carried in the contract; the selected carrier or harness owns concrete commands, files, enforcement, and executor topology.

Select a protection when omitting it could permit semantic drift, false completion, irreversible loss, or unbounded retries:

- **Reality and baseline** — for repo-changing work, carry the verified target state and task-relevant invariants. Carry a measured baseline and source when completion compares, preserves, migrates, regresses, counts, or claims performance; otherwise carry the first safe check and what a mismatch blocks.
- **Write boundary** — select when multiple edit surfaces, shared state, unrelated changes, or likely scope drift exist. Carry verified writable paths or owned seams and preserve everything else.
- **Autonomy** — select for unattended work. Carry every necessary reversible default with evidence state and cost of error; surface user-owned direction changes before execution.
- **Proof and reverse validation** — carry the smallest verified gate that exercises the requested outcome. Freeze the applicable gate or judging rule in Boundary; carry its required result and evidence in Success / Stop. Require a known-failure or red-to-green check when the gate is new, changed, or could pass without the behavior; the carrier owns exact commands and restoration steps.
- **Integrity** — select when writable tests, thresholds, fixtures, mocks, or other proxies offer an easier false-green route than completing the work. Freeze the relevant evidence and name only plausible prohibited shortcuts; the carrier owns enforcement.
- **Continuity** — select when work may outlive one context window. Carry the resumable objective, completed evidence, next action, and unresolved dependency; the carrier chooses the storage protocol.
- **Stop-loss and recovery** — select when retries can waste substantial effort or an attempt can degrade, destroy, or irreversibly change a verified state. Carry the stop threshold, last acceptable baseline, recovery expectation, and honest blocked outcome.
- **Independent evaluation** — select when the executor can self-certify, the result is subjective or proxy-gameable, or failure cost warrants separation. Carry the outcome and evidence an independent verifier must judge; the carrier chooses the verifier.
- **Parallel safety** — only with user authorization. Carry non-overlapping writes, an owned integration seam, and post-integration proof.

Execution work closes on an achieved result with proportional evidence. Exploration closes on a bounded decision or finding, not fake implementation metrics.

Place verified reality and baselines in Current Context; write scope, defaults, frozen judging rules, and anti-shortcut or integrity invariants in Boundary; preconditions and continuity in Immediate Task; and required proof results, reverse-validation evidence, stop-loss, recovery, and independent evaluation in Success / Stop.

Never invent a command or number. Do not accept a degraded result as completion, and do not treat a build as behavior proof when replay, trace, runtime, or contract evidence owns correctness.
