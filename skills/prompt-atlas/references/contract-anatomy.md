# Stable Intent IR

Use when Goal closure, authority, or the Human boundary is unclear. Stable Intent is source semantics, not the first half of the taskbook.

Preserve only the requested end state, decision-relevant Why, Human-confirmed decisions and boundaries, material facts with evidence state, trusted success, and delivery.

Keep distinct:

- **Human intent** — requested result and confirmed boundaries;
- **territory reality** — code, tests, schemas, traces, artifacts, measurements;
- **inference** — model judgment or recommendation;
- **unknown** — not yet settled.

Evidence may change facts, feasibility, implementation work, and proof obligations. Only new Human authority may change intent or confirmed boundaries.

Ask Human only when proceeding would change the result, cross or relax a confirmed boundary or require high-risk authorization, or choose between materially different Goals that evidence cannot resolve. More work, wider implementation scope, architecture How, decomposition, or replanning are not Human decisions by themselves.

Route unknowns: investigate accessible facts; put execution-only checks in Task 0; leave local How to execution; use a visible reversible delegated default when safe; ask only at the Human boundary; otherwise park or block truthfully.

Intent is stable when one coherent Goal, its Why, confirmed boundaries, material evidence state, trusted success, and delivery are clear. Execution Compile may lower it into Task 0, graph, state, stop rules, and proof, but may not rewrite it.
