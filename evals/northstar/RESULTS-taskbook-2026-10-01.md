## Limits

are not exported, so final artifacts cannot prove every reported call or exclude
transient edits. The captured intermediate rejection and external oracle are
stronger evidence than a final self-report, but do not validate a production
scheduler or transport. Automatic Skill routing, real-service backends and the
broader Phase 1 behavioral cases were not rerun here. The unchanged Phase 1 scope
boundary was reviewed in source. Publication stays in the same Draft PR.
