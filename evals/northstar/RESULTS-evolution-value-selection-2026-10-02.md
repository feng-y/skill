Source conformance does not mean the original improvement outcome was achieved, and lack of separation does not prove baseline already satisfies it. Keep the unmerged branch as an inspectable experiment, not as a claimed behavior improvement.

## Remaining limits and stopping decision

The comparison does not test implementation transfer, realized user outcomes, broad domain generalization, automatic routing, production priority or sustained multi-session execution. Local no-op does not have its own matched case; the broad plans allow no-change but did not stop the whole request as a no-op. Do not infer tested no-op reliability from wording alone.

The predeclared decision was to preserve current main if no consequential practical gain appeared. That condition holds in both broad pairs. Keep this branch and negative evidence for review; do not merge or add further runtime reminders, cases or a scorer to search for a favorable result. A future change should be motivated by new discriminating task evidence, not by the mere existence of this implementation.
